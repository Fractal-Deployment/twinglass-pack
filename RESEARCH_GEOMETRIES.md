# TwinGlass research geometries — canonical

This file is the canonical geometry map. Do not infer geometry from legacy filenames.

## Governing rule

**Research creates disagreement; disagreement does not create research.**

Agents follow evidence. They are not assigned thesis/antithesis roles and are not sent out to falsify one another. Falsification, replacement, or synthesis may occur only after independently gathered evidence is brought together.

## Invariant outside integrity pair

Every research geometry is watched by two outside agents that do **not** occupy research width:

1. **Semantic Integrity** — locked terms, referents, scope, inferential drift, non-contradiction.
2. **Data Integrity / LCD** — what the acquired data actually holds, provenance/function-set integrity, duplicate or incomparable evidence, claimed-not-held functions.

The pair may issue HOLD / REDIRECT / SOURCE-ANCHOR / PAIR-CANDIDATE signals. It does not choose the research conclusion and does not assign an antithesis.

A clean debate requires both:
- semantic integrity clear;
- data integrity clear.

## 1. Research Diamond — one bounded evidence round

A Diamond is one bounded round around one research charge.

- Start from one charge.
- Send independent evidence-gathering legs down **divergent but legitimate** routes.
- Routes are not opposites and are not proof/disproof teams.
- Each leg gathers the strongest real evidence on its route.
- When the round has enough information, bring the evidence packets together once.
- Only at convergence may the result be synthesis, replacement, falsification, or unresolved continuation.

The Diamond is **not** the tetrahedral self-critique.

## 2. Local research lattice — branching inside a leg

A research leg may discover multiple useful subpaths while following evidence. That local branching structure is a lattice inside the enclosing geometry. It is descriptive of the research trajectory, not a debate architecture.

## 3. Hourglass — persistent two-direction research with repeated constrictions

An Hourglass starts with two persistent research agents/directions.

Each cycle:
1. both agents research outward independently;
2. the outside integrity pair watches both pads;
3. when a real comparison is ready, the outside integrity pair classifies the relation; if it is a genuine debate path, both agents independently run the pre-debate tetrahedral self-critique;
4. the integrity pair clears the evidence packets; after a debate-path tetrahedron it clears the cleaned packets again;
5. the agents meet for **DEBATE or SYNTHESIS**;
6. both agents go outward again:
   - after debate, each strengthens/revises its own account against the discovered incompatibility;
   - after synthesis, each follows new divergent implications of the synthesis.

Default planning horizon is roughly 4–5 meet cycles, but stop earlier when sufficient information closes the pair. If one direction reaches sufficient closure, the paired run ends for both.

The two starting directions are not assigned opposites.

## 4. Spectral lattice — emergent recursive research topology

The Spectral lattice starts with one researcher and one research topic.

- The researcher follows evidence on the live path.
- When evidence reveals a sufficiently distinct research route, write an evidence-backed divergence note.
- Spawn another isolated researcher onto that route.
- Parent keeps walking.
- Spawned researchers may discover and spawn further evidence-backed routes.
- The topology is created by the research itself; it is not preassigned.

A legal spawn requires:
- `divergenceEvidence` — quoted evidence that a distinct route exists;
- `otherTrackEvidence` — quoted evidence naming the distinct route/function-set;
- `necessaryBecause` — why separating the route preserves the research telos;
- the new route is not merely a synonym of a live lane;
- the new route is not an assigned antithesis.

The current parent path does **not** have to be wrong or improper.

## 5. Pairing in a spectral lattice

The outside integrity pair can see relations that isolated researchers cannot. It may flag two pads as a **PAIR-CANDIDATE** when their acquired evidence is genuinely complementary or incompatible.

The orchestrator decides whether to pair. Pairing does not itself mean debate.

## 6. Pre-debate 3D tetrahedral self-critique

The tetrahedron runs **only when an agent is preparing to enter a debate**. Complementary synthesis does not require a tetrahedron merely to create symmetry.

Each paired agent runs it on its **own** pad before seeing the other agent's cleaned argument:

- T1 — bare data / direct inscription;
- T2 — literature or narrative framing;
- T3 — model prior / pre-training / RAG echo audit;
- T4 — current causal or phase-space account.

The six cross-track relations test the agent against itself.

**Tetrahedron = self-comparison, not opponent-comparison.**

After each agent completes the tetrahedron, the outside Semantic Integrity + Data Integrity pair checks the cleaned packet again. Only then is it debate-eligible.

Canonical order:

```text
research
  -> evidence-backed divergence as needed
  -> outside pairing decision
  -> semantic + data integrity classification/clearance
  -> if complementary: synthesis
  -> if genuinely incompatible: each agent independently self-critiques
       -> semantic + data integrity re-clearance
       -> debate
  -> downstream adjudication
```

## 7. Falsification placement

No research agent is assigned a falsifier and no retrieval planner searches merely to kill a branch.

Predictions may guide where to look, but missing or contrary evidence is recorded as evidence first.

Falsification is a possible **downstream adjudication result after evidence has been gathered and compared**. It is never the generative research instruction.

## 8. Legacy implementation names

Some engine/file names predate this correction:
- `engine/diamond-engine.ts` is a legacy internal-critique substrate. It is **not** the canonical Research Diamond.
- `diamondId` / `in-diamond` are compatibility field/state names in the current engine. Treat them as pre-debate critique mutex state until renamed in a dedicated compatibility migration.
- `improperEvidence` is a retired spawn concept. Current canonical spawn evidence is `divergenceEvidence`.

Do not let those legacy names redefine the geometry above.
