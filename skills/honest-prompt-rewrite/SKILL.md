# honest-prompt-rewrite

**One job:** rewrite the working prompt under LRR. This is the shared gate. Lattice and twin consume it. They do not fork it.

`measured_omega=false` · no invent-green

## Three laws

1. Identity — a locked term keeps one referent. New sense, new name.
2. Non-contradiction — do not hold A and not-A under the same lock.
3. Excluded middle — do not hide the split in a vague third.

## Each round

1. **Logic** — same term, same referent. If a new sense is needed, mint a new name.
2. **Ration** — mint short pattern markers from residue. Do not narrate them away.
3. **Reason** — keep a claim only with `necessary_because` (telos link) AND a marker.

Telos does not move. The working prompt may.

## Do not

- Load `morph-shared` as well. That folder is an alias. Dead.
- Close product seals from a fluent paragraph.
- Become the lattice or the twin.

Grok.com: Twin lock phase. CLI: this folder. `/activate` in this pack loads this skill, nothing else.
