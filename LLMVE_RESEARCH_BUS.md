# LLMVE variable-centered research bus

**Cross-repository blueprint:** https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cBlBFB3h2-Gs5rngfIW--iKDae57TaNzZrBPtFnPomI/edit

This document binds TwinGlass to the LLMVE Agent Research Kernel without moving LLMVE meaning into this repository.

## Ownership

- `llmve-meaning` owns variable meaning and authority.
- TwinGlass owns research geometry and epistemic gates.
- `llmve-agent-supervisor` owns durable execution and physical resource leases.
- `llmve-agent-autonomy` owns autonomous-vs-human escalation policy.

## Research bus

Agents may research in parallel, but contributions are admitted in a deterministic sequence after the outside Semantic Integrity and Data Integrity/LCD pair clears them. Live sibling pads remain isolated.

A contribution addresses current LLMVE variables, relations, word problems, observations, or anomalies. This repository must not hard-code a historical variable roster as authority.

## Comparison gate

A detected relationship may create a constriction/comparison point. Before any cross-pad interaction:

1. every participant executes its own tetrahedral self-critique;
2. the outside SI + Data Integrity pair re-clears the cleaned material;
3. the meet opens with the exact relationship and admitted evidence;
4. the result is discovered as synthesis, replacement, debate, or unresolved.

Debate is never assigned at birth.

## Unresolved result

An unresolved meet emits a discriminating research or experiment request rather than continuing conversation indefinitely. Physical execution is owned by the resource broker in `llmve-agent-supervisor`.

## Code

`engine/research-bus.ts` contains the deterministic contracts and gates. It deliberately does not:
- decide truth;
- redefine LLMVE variables;
- perform retrieval;
- read sibling live histories;
- schedule CPU/GPU resources.
